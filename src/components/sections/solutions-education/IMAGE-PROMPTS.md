## `education-hero-mockup.png`

```text
Use case: product-mockup
Asset type: premium transparent hero product mockup for the Dealup education industry landing page
Primary request: Create one cohesive device composition: a slim graphite laptop at a slight three-quarter angle showing a modern, shippable Dealup education admissions command center, with one smartphone standing in front showing an original learner conversation and application checklist.
Input images: Image 1 is the exact Dealup brand lockup reference. Preserve its identity, silhouette, two-tone forest-green/bright-green colors, lowercase wordmark proportions, and spelling. It may appear exactly once, small and tasteful, in the laptop interface header only; do not redesign it.
Scene/backdrop: genuinely transparent background with true alpha everywhere outside the devices and their soft grounding shadows. No color field, no gradient panel, no pedestal, no room, no decorative backdrop, and no enclosing card.
Laptop screen: polished high-fidelity admissions command center UI with calm warm-white surfaces and restrained forest-green/mint accents. Left navigation and practical content areas for “New enquiries”, “Programmes”, “Applications”, “Events”, and “Advisor handoffs”. Show a believable enquiry list using Moroccan names such as Lina Amrani, Youssef El Idrissi, and Sara Benali; programme references such as “Business Analytics”, “Digital Marketing”, and “Licence Informatique”; statuses such as “New enquiry”, “Checklist ready”, and “Advisor assigned”. Do not show charts with invented performance numbers and do not show marketing claims.
Smartphone screen: original green-and-white chat interface with no third-party branding. A learner asks about the “Business Analytics” programme; the reply presents a concise application checklist with “Transcript”, “ID copy”, and “Motivation letter”, plus a small “Talk to an advisor” action. Keep the conversation readable at hero scale.
Style/medium: premium photorealistic 3D hardware mockup combined with realistic shippable product UI, not concept art. Crisp graphite aluminium, subtle reflections, accurate screens and clean anti-aliased edges.
Composition/framing: landscape transparent cutout, laptop is the dominant object angled slightly in perspective, smartphone overlaps the lower front edge without hiding the most important dashboard content; both complete devices fully visible with generous transparent margins. Soft realistic contact shadows only beneath devices.
Lighting/mood: controlled soft studio lighting, premium trustworthy education-technology mood, gentle highlights with no dramatic glow.
Color palette: graphite, warm white, Dealup forest green, bright leaf green and pale mint. Use no blue-heavy palette.
Text: render only the short interface labels listed above. Preserve the exact brand spelling “dealup” if the logo is rendered.
Constraints: one laptop and one smartphone only; genuine alpha outside devices; logo no more than once and only if faithful to Image 1; no fake metrics, percentages, testimonials, or performance claims; no WhatsApp logo or recognizable WhatsApp trade dress; no other brand logos; no watermark; no decorative background; no card behind the devices; no floating badges outside screens.
Avoid: gibberish text, duplicate phones, cropped hardware, distorted keyboards, extra devices, arbitrary charts, illustration style, green haze, large brand marks, visual clutter.
```
